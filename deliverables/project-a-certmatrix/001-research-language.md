# CertMatrix — Market language research (training matrix / cert tracking)

Date: 2026-02-02

## Summary (what people keep saying)
The repeated pain themes (across construction training-matrix vendors + safety resources):
- **Audit pressure:** “compliance audits”, “proof of training”, “audit trail”, “inspection readiness”.
- **Spreadsheet fatigue:** training records “fall out of date”, “manual Excel trackers”, “chasing paperwork”, “email chains”.
- **Expiry risk:** “certifications … set to expire”, “expiry alerts”, “renewal dates”, “no one turns up to site without valid credentials”.
- **Multi-sub complexity:** “multiple subcontractors”, “grouped by subcontractor”, “submit up-to-date training records” to GCs/clients.

## 4) Recommendations: ad angles
### Angle A — “GC audit / prequal panic”
- Hook: You get the email: “Send your training matrix by EOD.”
- Promise: Generate a GC-ready training matrix PDF in 30 seconds.
- Proof language: “audit readiness”, “proof of training”, “grouped by subcontractor”.

### Angle B — “expiry risk / stopped at the gate”
- Hook: One expired card = worker can’t step on site.
- Promise: Expiry tracking + reminders so “no one turns up to site without valid credentials.”
- Outcome: avoid delays + rework + embarrassment.
