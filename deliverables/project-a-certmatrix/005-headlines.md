# CertMatrix — Headline variants (mapped to traffic source)

Goal: match the promise to the intent.
- **Google Search intent:** “training matrix / compliance / prequal / audit / cert tracking” → wants *fast proof + exact deliverable*.
- **Facebook cold traffic:** “admin chaos / GC pressure / scrambling” → wants *relief + story + credibility*.

## A) Google Search (high intent)
Use for keywords like: `training matrix`, `training register`, `competency matrix`, `proof of training`, `compliance audit`, `cert tracking`, `expiration alerts`.

1) **Generate a GC‑Ready Training Matrix PDF in 30 Seconds**
   - Subhead: Stop scrambling for proof of training. Export a clean matrix anytime the GC asks.

2) **Construction Training Matrix Software for Subcontractors**
   - Subhead: Track OSHA/CPR/Fall/Rigging renewals + export audit-ready PDFs on demand.

3) **Proof of Training—Ready in Seconds (Not After 6 Email Chains)**
   - Subhead: One click creates a formatted matrix you can send to the GC/client.

4) **Know Who’s Compliant, Who’s Overdue, and What’s About to Expire**
   - Subhead: Alerts at 60/30/7 days so no one shows up with an expired card.

5) **Stop Managing Certs in Excel**
   - Subhead: Centralize training records, renewal dates, and exports—without broken spreadsheets.

6) **Training Register + Expiry Alerts Built for Jobsite Reality**
   - Subhead: Simple dashboard: green good / yellow expiring / red action needed.

## B) Facebook / cold traffic (pain-story angle)
Use for angles like: “GC just called”, “audit tomorrow”, “stopped at the gate”, “spreadsheet mess”.

7) **The GC Called. They Need Your Training Matrix by Tomorrow.**
   - Subhead: CertMatrix exports a GC-ready PDF in 30 seconds—no midnight spreadsheet surgery.

8) **One Expired Card Can Shut Down a Crew**
   - Subhead: Get renewal reminders before it becomes a jobsite problem.

9) **Your Training Spreadsheet Is Lying to You**
   - Subhead: Records fall out of date fast. CertMatrix keeps everything current automatically.

10) **Make Compliance Boring Again**
    - Subhead: Track training, catch expirations early, and send proof instantly when asked.

## C) “Free template” lead magnet (lower intent)
Use when pushing the template as the primary CTA (or as an alternative CTA).

11) **Free Training Matrix Template (Excel) + GC Audit Checklist**
    - Subhead: Download the template now—then upgrade when you’re tired of chasing cards.

12) **Start with the Template. Upgrade When You Want Alerts + 1‑Click PDFs**
    - Subhead: Use the free spreadsheet today; switch to CertMatrix when you want automation.
