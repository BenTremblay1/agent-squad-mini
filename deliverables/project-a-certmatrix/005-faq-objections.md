# CertMatrix — FAQ (objections + suggested answers)

Aim: answer the “yeah but…” questions that block a click/call.

## 1) “What certifications can I track?”
Track anything your crew needs (OSHA 10/30, First Aid/CPR, Fall Protection, Confined Space, Forklift, Rigging, Scaffold, Hazmat, etc.). You can also add custom certs that are specific to your trade or a particular GC/site.

## 2) “Can you import my existing spreadsheet?”
Yes. If you already track training in Excel/Sheets, we can import via CSV and map it once. Most teams are loaded in ~15–30 minutes.

## 3) “What does ‘GC‑ready’ mean—what will the PDF look like?”
A clean training matrix with each employee, required certs, status, and expiration/renewal dates—formatted so you can send it straight to a GC or client without reworking it.

## 4) “How do expiration alerts work?”
You’ll get alerts before a cert expires (e.g., 60/30/7 days). That way you schedule renewals before it becomes a jobsite emergency.

## 5) “We have multiple job sites / multiple GCs—can we filter by site or project?”
Yes (recommended). You can group/filter so you can produce the right matrix for the right job/site instead of maintaining multiple versions of the same spreadsheet.

## 6) “Is this overkill for a small crew?”
If you only have a handful of certs and they rarely change, a spreadsheet can work—until it doesn’t. CertMatrix is for when you’re tired of chasing cards, fixing versions, and scrambling when the GC asks for proof.

## 7) “Do you integrate with payroll/HR?”
Not yet. Today you can import/export via CSV. We’re prioritizing integrations based on what customers already use—tell us what system you’re on and we’ll put it in the queue.

## 8) “What if I need to cancel?”
No contracts. Cancel anytime. You can export your data so you’re not locked in.
