## Implementation Notes (quick)

- **Personalization that matters:** trade, region, headcount, GC names, upcoming job date.
- **Don’t over-automate:** keep “reply with A/B/C/D” and “reply ‘checklist’” as real replies (not forms) so it feels human.
- **Cadence:** avoid weekends unless you know the office is open.
- **Compliance:** include opt-out language on SMS if needed (e.g., “Reply STOP to opt out”). Consider adding it on the *first* SMS touch if you’re cold-texting.
